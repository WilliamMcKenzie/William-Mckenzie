AI Tools

AI tools can obviously be very productive when used correctly (boring & mind numbing stuff), but theres no such thing as a free lunch.

I see it like this: building without AI is like going to the gym and working out. As per the laws of delayed gratification, you get stronger and progress over time.

Building with AI is like bringing a industrial piston to the bench press, yeah no shit it'll lift more then you but the progression is flat. That isn't to say that you can't find ways to make it more efficient, positioning it differently etc (agents), but the actual pushing power will stay the same.

Now of course that is just in terms of when practicing/training, in real application you may not care about progression as long as it does the job.
